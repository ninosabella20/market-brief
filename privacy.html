Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Market Brief and Market Brief Podcast are personal projects operated by Nino Sabella. They publish daily stock-market sector summaries and automatically upload videos to the operator's own YouTube channel.

Data we collect

The public website does not collect, store or share personal data from visitors and does not use cookies or analytics. The automation connects only to the operator's own Google account through Google OAuth, solely to upload videos to the operator's own YouTube channel.

Google user data

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Access tokens are stored privately by the operator, are used only to upload videos, and are never shared, sold or used for advertising.
